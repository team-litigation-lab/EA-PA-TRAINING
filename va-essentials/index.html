Process Questions

  1. Analyze a hypothetical case: A breach of contract with parties in two states. Which court system would have jurisdiction? Why?
  2. Assess the advantages and disadvantages of pursuing a case in federal court instead of state court for a personal injury claim.
  3. Examine the core responsibilities of a Legal VA and compare them with those of an in-house legal assistant. What are the operational implications for the firm?
  4. Scrutinize the challenges that solo attorneys face without VA support. How does a Legal VA help resolve these?
  5. Evaluate how Legal VAs contribute to law firm efficiency and client satisfaction. Which benefit do you think has the greatest long-term impact, and why?
  6. Assess how firm size impacts the level of autonomy, multitasking, and specialization expected of a Legal VA.
  7. Appraise the effectiveness of formal vs. informal communication strategies when working with different types of attorneys.
  8. Analyze the key cultural values in U.S. professional settings (e.g., directness, time orientation, individualism) and compare them with typical Filipino and Latin American values.
  9. Provide a scenario where a VA’s culturally influenced approach leads to miscommunication with a U.S.-based attorney. What were the causes?
  10. Critique the statement: “Filipino and Latin American Legal VAs should completely adopt American communication styles to be effective.”
